# Trapper's Freight Elevators

Move items and fluids between factory floors with an extendable freight elevator.

**Release candidate: 0.1.13.** Prepared for a public beta. Requires Satisfactory build 513885 or newer and SML 3.12.0 compatible with the installed game. Gameplay validation was performed on build 513885; newer game builds are not automatically verified.

## Features

- Separate item and fluid elevators in three tiers.
- An 8 m × 8 m footprint, extendable shaft, and floor stops placed to suit your factory.
- Build the base and up to ten 4 m shaft sections together, then extend the tower later.
- Adjust stop placement in 1 m, 2 m or 4 m increments.
- Four numbered ports per stop, each configurable as input or output.
- Route one resource to multiple floors using delivery weights.
- Separate incoming and outgoing floor storage with independent reserved-stack rules.
- One, two or four isolated fluid compartments.
- Immediate pickup or wait-until-full with a per-floor maximum wait.
- Animated travel, landing gates, and Ironworks industrial styling.

Unlock Mk.1, Mk.2 and Mk.3 through the HUB at tiers 4, 6 and 8.
